Industry: AI & Computer Vision

My Role: UI/UX Designer, Graphic Designer

Timeline: 4 Weeks

Platform: Web / Mobile

/Context

What am I designing?

Layman AI is a marketing website for an AI and computer vision solutions company. The goal was to present complex technology in a way that feels clear, modern, and approachable for businesses across different industries.

Role: UI/UX Designer & Graphic Designer, with end-to-end ownership across product strategy, UX/UI design, brand language, and visual identity.

/Challenge

What problem am I solving?

AI and computer vision solutions are hard for non-technical visitors to understand, explore, and trust. There was no clear path from interest to enquiry.

/Design response

How does the design answer it?

Each problem is answered directly in the interface.

  • Complex technology→Simple, approachable languageHomepage · Section copy
  • Hard to explore→Clear product structure + filtersProduct cards · Category filters
  • Low visual proof→Demos, stats & testimonialsProduct pages · Homepage
  • Unclear next step→Consistent CTAs throughoutEvery page · Consultation path
